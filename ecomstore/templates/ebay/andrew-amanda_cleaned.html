We ship to most countries around the world.  For international orders, we offer First Class International (3-8 weeks), Priority Mail Intenational (6-14 days) and Priority Mail Express International (5-9 days). First class international mail is the least expensive but is only trackable within the US; once a First Class International Parcel leaves a US port, it enters the Customs of the destination country, it is not trackable outside of US. Lost packages are rare, but long delays by Customs are quite often in some countries, especially in Russia, Brazil and Italy.

The non-flat-rate Priority mail international and the Priority Mail Express International are trackable to the destinations in most of the countries.
