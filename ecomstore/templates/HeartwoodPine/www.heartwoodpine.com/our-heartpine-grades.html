Antique Quartersawn Heartpine

Simply the best of the best—hand-selected and made from 100% longleaf heartwood pine. Our quartersawn wood flooring is painstakingly reclaimed to its original beauty in our Chatham County, NC facility. The rich burgundy colors and tight linear grain patterns evoke the wood’s true character and history. For more info about quartersawn heart pine in Moncure, NC, call us today!

View More

Select #1 Heartpine

Our Select #1 heartpine features all the beauty and integrity of our antique heartpine with a distinct grain patterns and warm tones create an inviting atmosphere that looks great in any room. Perfect for when you want the gorgeous look of antique honey, amber and burgundy heartpine for your home or office space!

Natural Antique Heartpine

If making a big impression is what you have in mind, this wide-grained heartwood is sure to bring attention to any room. No matter what your decorating style, natural antique heartpine is the right choice that will add character to your home and be enjoyed for generations.

Character Heartpine

Our durable Character Heartpine is called that because each and every board hand-selected by us is a testament to this amazing wood’s journey from ancient forest, to factory, then remilled and ready for your home or office.

View More

Antique Southern Yellow Pine

Full of character and a style unique to the timber, our most affordable antique Southern Yellow Heartpine has a golden hue and high stain ability that make it an ideal choice as reclaimed wood flooring for a wide variety of home or office spaces.
