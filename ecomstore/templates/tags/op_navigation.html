    How to make an offer From Name Your Price widgetClick the Make Your Offer link on the top right corner in this panel to launch the NameYourPrice widget. You can search and add multiple products into the offer basket and make one bulk offer From product pageFind your product. Click on the Make Offer button. It will add the product to the offer basket. You will have the opportunity to add more products and make a bulk offer From shopping cartAdd products into your shopping cart. You can make an offer for all the products in your shopping cart
    Frequently asked questions Do I need an account to make an offer?Yes, each offer is registered and tracked under a unique email address. Your email is used to communicate the status change of the offer to you.
    You can also login using your existing social accounts, such Amazon, gmail, yahoo, facebook, etc     Can I make an offer for multiple products at one time?Absolutely. That is the unique feature we offer to our customers. You can add multiple products into your offer basket and enter a combined offer price. What can I expect after I enter an offer?You should receive an email communication when your offer is accepted or if your offer is countered by a new price. This should happen within 12 hours. How do I find out if my offer is accepted or countered?You will receive an email notification when the status of your offer is changed;
    Or you can click or refresh your offer page on the site to check on the status of your offer     How do counter offers work?You can click on your offer and enter a new reasonable amount for your offer. Click Submit,
    Your counter offer will be submited to the seller for review, much like how eBay's Make Offer works     Can I take back an offer?Yes, you can cancel an offer at any time before you have made the payment for an accepted offer How many offer can I make at one time?One. At any given time, you can only have one outstanding offer. You must either clear the payment for an accepted offer or cancel an existing offer before you enter a new offer Can I add or remove a product from an offer I have entered? Can I change the quantity?Once an offer is entered, you can not update the products or quantity. But you can easily cancel the offer and re-enter a new one.
