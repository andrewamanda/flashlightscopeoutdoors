Finest Antique Reclaimed Wood Floors in Moncure, NC

Heartwood Pine Floors & Beyond Inc has been crafting elegant antique heart pine floors in Moncure, NC. Since 1982, we have been carefully reclaiming mighty timbers from eighteenth-century industrial buildings that were destined for demolition. Beams cut from 400-year-old longleaf pines are recycled into antique heartwood pine flooring at our millworks in Chatham County, North Carolina, and shipped factory direct to our customers. Over the years, we have been providing floors milled from antique American Natural Pines and Oaks, which have now become our main offerings

Our History

Heart pine is the finest lumber ever grown. Early American settlers discovered a vast forest of over 95 million acres spanning from the Southern Atlantic shoreline to the Mississippi River. This forest grew trees four feet in diameter and up to 150 feet tall. These slow-growth heartwood pine trees matured in 400 to 500 years.
