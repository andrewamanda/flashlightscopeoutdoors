Pricing Explained

{% endblock %} {% block pricing_table %}

At Tarheel Reclaimed, we're committed to providing you with the finest quality reclaimed hardwood floors. Over time, we've seen a shift in manufacturing costs due to various factors, including the scarcity of reclaimed wood. As a result, we need to update our pricing structure to ensure that we can continue to offer you the best products and service.

New Pricing Structure: Our updated pricing structure is designed to reflect the evolving landscape of reclaimed hardwood manufacturing. We understand that our customers have diverse needs, and we want to accommodate both small and large orders. Here's an overview of our new pricing tiers:

  1. Standard Pricing:

    • This tier is ideal for customers with smaller orders or specific requirements. While prices have been adjusted to account for increased manufacturing costs, it remains competitive within the market.

  2. Volume Pricing:

    • For larger orders, we offer volume pricing that provides cost relief. We recognize that larger orders can help us streamline our manufacturing processes, which results in savings that we're happy to pass on to you.

Why Volume Pricing? Our decision to offer volume pricing is driven by the nature of our manufacturing process. Reclaimed hardwood floors require specialized adjustments and priming of equipment and machines. These setup costs are significant, especially for smaller orders. By encouraging larger volume orders, we can better optimize our production processes, making it more efficient and cost-effective for everyone.

Custom Solutions: At Tarheel Reclaimed, we understand that each project is unique. If you have specific requirements or need a tailored solution, please don't hesitate to reach out to our dedicated customer support team. We're here to assist you in finding the perfect flooring solution that fits your needs and budget.

While we understand that pricing adjustments are never easy, they are necessary to maintain the high standards of quality and service that you expect from Tarheel Reclaimed. We appreciate your understanding and continued support as we navigate these changes together.

If you have any questions or would like to discuss your specific project needs, please feel free to contact us. Thank you for choosing Tarheel Reclaimed as your trusted partner for reclaimed hardwood flooring.
