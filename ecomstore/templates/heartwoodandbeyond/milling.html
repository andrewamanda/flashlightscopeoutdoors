Custom Milling Service

At Tar Heel Reclaimed , we breathe new life into aged and weathered wooden materials, transforming them into stunning reclaimed flooring that brims with character and history. Our custom milling service is a doorway to sustainable luxury, offering you a chance to own a piece of timeless elegance crafted from deckings, beams, old floorboards, and more.

 

How It Works

Our process is an alchemy of tradition and technology, involving several carefully mastered steps to ensure the final product not only meets but exceeds your expectations:

  1. Dry Kilning: All materials undergo dry kilning, a method that eradicates moisture and pests, laying a strong foundation for durability and longevity.
  2. Band Sawing: We utilize band sawing to cut the materials into desired shapes and sizes, maintaining the intrinsic qualities while removing imperfections.
  3. Edging: Our experts meticulously edge each piece, ensuring uniformity and creating a neat appearance that facilitates seamless installation.
  4. Cutting Tongue and Grooves:  The cutting of tongue and grooves is a precise endeavor, which grants each flooring piece a snug fit, promising a floor that’s both visually appealing and structurally sound.
