Order Shipping

We ship all orders within one business day, except for the items which are back ordered. Majority of orders are shipped by the United Postal Service.

Product Returning

Products can be returned for a full refund within 30 days of purchases, assuming products are in new and unused condition. An Return Material Authorization (RMA) number must be obtained prior to the return.

Product Warranty

We offer free exchange for any defective products within 30 days of purchase; After 30 days, warranty services are provided by the manfacturers. For products manufactured overseas, we help our customers with shipping to the manufacturers with a minimal charge to save our customers the expensive international shipping cost.
